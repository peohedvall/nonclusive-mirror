About this site

This is my personal website, a place to write and think aloud, discuss my work, share resources, and connect dots together with people who share my passion for Universal Design. If you share my interest in how we can design for a better world, this site is for you. I hope you find value here. I am by no means alone in this. Most of what I write about grew out of research with colleagues, co-authors, doctoral students, partner municipalities and disability organisations, and it continues in the projects I lead and take part in. Some of the people I work with are named there and in my CV.

Three reasons I write online:

  • To experiment with new ways to write, and make a few friends along the way.
  • To learn together with others in public as I keep exploring how to work as an academic.
  • To share resources and talk about our research from a more personal perspective, grounded in my own experiences.

Short formal description

Per-Olof Hedvall, PhD, is a Professor of Design at Mid Sweden University and Bevica Guest Professor in Universal Design at Kolding School of Design, Denmark. His research examines categorisations of “us” and “them” in design and policy, and how Accessibility, Usability, and Universal Design can help design move beyond that sorting. A recurring theme is how design tends to centre a norm and treat human variation as deviation. In response, he and his colleagues developed the concept of nonclusion, design that resists categorising people and comes with no predefined limits on who it is meant for.
