Photo collection: Wheelchair signs from around the world

Each sign is a categorisation you can see. I photograph them wherever I travel. Look at what each one depicts, a person or a function.

What it is

I photograph wheelchair signs wherever I travel. Not because the signs are wrong, but because each one is a small public record of how a society categorises: who a space expects, who gets a special route, who is depicted and who is assumed.

Look at what each sign depicts. Some show a person. Then the space is for a kind of body, and the categories tend to multiply, from one symbol to three, to six. Some show a function instead, a multi-purpose space, a door, a slope. In my experience the shift from person to function is the easiest nonclusive move to explain, and these photographs are how I explain it.

The photographs come from wherever I have travelled, and the collection keeps growing.
