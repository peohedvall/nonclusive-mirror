Open resources

Diagrams, photos, and one-pagers, free to use.

I do a lot of presentations, inside and outside academia, and I am often asked whether people can use the photos and illustrations I make. The answer is always yes. Here I gather the material I use most. Take it into your teaching, a talk, or a video. I would love to hear how you use it.
